The cool-down is arguably the most important part for **long-term consistency**. It shifts your body from "fight or flight" (exercise mode) back into "rest and digest" (recovery mode), which helps reduce that second-day muscle soreness.

Unlike the warm-up, these are **static stretches**. Hold each one for **30–45 seconds** and focus on deep, belly breathing.

---

### 🧘 The 3-Minute "Reset" Cool Down

| Order | Stretch | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Child’s Pose** | Lower back, shoulders, and hips. |
| **02** | **Cobra/Upward Dog** | Abdominals and chest (great after Core/Upper days). |
| **03** | **Lunge with Reach** | Hip flexors (crucial if you sit at a desk all day). |
| **04** | **Figure-4 Stretch** | Glutes and outer hips (great after Lower Body days). |

---

### How to do them:

* **Child’s Pose:** Kneel on the floor, sit back on your heels, and reach your arms forward on the ground. Breathe into your lower back.
* **Cobra:** Lie on your stomach, hands under shoulders, and gently press up to stretch your stomach. If your back feels "pinched," stay low on your elbows.
* **Lunge with Reach:** Kneel on one knee (other foot flat in front). Squeeze the glute of the kneeling leg and reach that same arm overhead, leaning slightly away from the kneeling side.
* **Figure-4 Stretch:** Lie on your back. Cross your right ankle over your left knee. Pull your left thigh toward your chest. You’ll feel this in your right glute. Switch sides.

### 🌟 The "Golden Hour" Tip
Try to drink a glass of water and have a small snack with protein (like Greek yogurt, a handful of nuts, or a protein shake) within 60 minutes of finishing. This gives your muscles the raw materials they need to "rebuild" stronger than they were before the workout.

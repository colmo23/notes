# 🏆 The 15-Minute Daily Habit
**The Flow:** 3-Min Warm-Up ➔ 10-Min Circuit (45s Work / 15s Rest) ➔ 2-Min Cool Down

## 📅 The Weekly Rotation
| Day | Routine | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mon / Thu** | **Routine A: Core** | Stability, Posture, Abs |
| **Tue / Fri** | **Routine B: Lower** | Glutes, Quads, Hips |
| **Wed / Sat** | **Routine C: Upper** | Chest, Back, Arms |
| **Sunday** | **Active Recovery** | Walking, Stretching, or Rest |

---

## 🚀 Step 1: 3-Minute Warm-Up (Every Day)
*30 seconds each, no rest:*
1. **Arm Circles:** (15s forward / 15s back).
2. **Cat-Cow:** On all fours, arch and round your spine.
3. **Bodyweight Hinges:** Bow forward with a flat back to prep hamstrings.
4. **Alt. Knee Hugs:** Pull knees to chest while standing.
5. **Torso Twists:** Rotate your ribs side-to-side.
6. **Marching High Knees:** Get the blood pumping.

---

## ⚡ Step 2: The 10-Minute Circuits
*Pick the routine for the day. Perform 2 rounds of the 5 moves below.*

### **Routine A: Core Foundation**
1. **Dead Bug:** Lower back flat, alternate opposite arm/leg.
2. **Bird-Dog:** Opposite arm/leg extension from all fours.
3. **Forearm Plank:** Straight line from head to heels.
4. **Bicycle Crunches:** Slow, elbow-to-opposite-knee rotation.
5. **Side Plank:** 22s per side (keep hips high).

### **Routine B: Power Base (Lower Body)**
1. **Bodyweight Squat:** Sit back into heels, chest up.
2. **Reverse Lunge:** Step back, both knees at $90^\circ$.
3. **Glute Bridge:** Lift hips toward ceiling, squeeze glutes.
4. **Lateral Lunge:** Big step to the side, keep trailing leg straight.
5. **Calf Raises:** Rise high on toes, control the way down.

### **Routine C: Upper Body & Posture**
1. **Push-Ups:** Hands on floor or elevated on a couch.
2. **Superman Pulldowns:** Lie face down, pull elbows to ribs.
3. **Tricep Dips:** Use a chair or couch edge.
4. **"I-Y-W" Raises:** Lie face down, lift arms in those letter shapes.
5. **Incline Push-Ups:** Hands on couch, feet on floor (burnout set).

---

## 🧘 Step 3: 2-Minute Cool Down (Every Day)
*Hold each for 30 seconds:*
1. **Child’s Pose:** Kneel and reach forward (Relaxes back/shoulders).
2. **Cobra:** Lie on stomach, press chest up (Stretches abs).
3. **Lunge with Reach:** Kneel on one knee, reach same-side arm up (Stretches hips).
4. **Figure-4 Stretch:** Cross ankle over opposite knee while lying down (Stretches glutes).

---

### 💡 Final Success Tip
If you miss a day, **don't double up the next day.** Just pick up where you left off. Consistency beats intensity every single time.

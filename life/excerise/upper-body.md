To round out your full-body plan, we need to target the "push" and "pull" muscles of the upper body. Doing these at home without a gym can be tricky for the back muscles, but we can use body weight and gravity to get it done.

Here is the final piece of your 10-minute home workout puzzle.

---

## 🟨 Routine C: Upper Body & Posture
*Format: 45 Seconds Work | 15 Seconds Rest | 2 Rounds Total (10 Mins)*

| Order | Exercise | Target Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Push-Ups** | Chest, Shoulders, & Triceps |
| **02** | **Superman Pulldowns** | Upper Back & Lats (Posture!) |
| **03** | **Tricep Dips** | Back of the Arms |
| **04** | **"I-Y-W" Raises** | Rear Delts & Traps |
| **05** | **Incline Push-Ups** | Lower Chest & Endurance |

---

### How to do these correctly:

* **Push-Ups:** If a full push-up is too hard, drop to your knees or place your hands on a elevated surface (like a couch). Keep your elbows at a **45° angle** from your body—don't let them flare out like a "T."
* **Superman Pulldowns:** Lie face down on the floor. Lift your chest and arms slightly. Mimic a "pull-up" motion by pulling your elbows down toward your ribs, squeezing your shoulder blades together hard.
* **Tricep Dips:** Use a sturdy chair or the edge of your couch. Keep your hips close to the chair as you lower yourself. Only go down until your elbows are at **90°**.
* **"I-Y-W" Raises:** Lie face down. Lift your arms to form an "I", then a "Y", then bend elbows for a "W". This is the best move for fixing "computer slouch" posture.
* **Incline Push-Ups:** Place your hands on a chair or couch and feet on the floor. This is a bit easier than floor push-ups and helps you finish the circuit with good form.

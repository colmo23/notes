A proper warm-up is like "priming the engine." It signals to your nervous system that it’s time to move and increases blood flow to your joints, which significantly reduces the risk of strains.

Since your workouts are 10 minutes, this **3-minute "Dynamic Flow"** is designed to get you ready without wearing you out. Perform each move for **30 seconds** with no rest in between.

---

### 🕒 The 3-Minute Quick Start

| Order | Exercise | What it Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Arm Circles** | Lubricates the shoulder joints. (15s forward, 15s back) |
| **02** | **Cat-Cow** | Wakes up the spine and core. (On all fours, arch and round back) |
| **03** | **Bodyweight Hinges** | Preps the hamstrings and lower back. (Think "bowing") |
| **04** | **Alt. Knee Hugs** | Opens up the hips and glutes. |
| **05** | **Torso Twists** | Releases tension in the mid-back (thoracic spine). |
| **06** | **High Knees (Marching)** | Gets the heart rate up and blood flowing to the legs. |

---

### ⚠️ Two Golden Rules of Warming Up
1.  **Never Static Stretch:** Avoid holding a stretch for 30+ seconds before your workout. It actually "turns off" the muscle slightly. Save the long holds for *after* the workout.
2.  **Gradual Intensity:** Start the first 30 seconds very gently and increase your range of motion as you get to the final minute.
